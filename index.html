Zentix Team built the "Z Pinger: Network Stabilizer" app as a Free/Freemium app. This SERVICE is provided by Zentix Team and is intended for use "as is". This page is used to inform visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our Service.

Information Collection and Use

We want to be transparent: Z Pinger does not collect, store, or share any direct personally identifiable information (PII) like names, emails, or phone numbers. The app primarily operates locally on your device. We do not track your location, read your files, or access your contacts.

However, to support the development of this app, we use third-party advertising services (such as Unity Ads and other advertising partners). These third-party services may automatically collect non-personally identifiable information, specifically Device or other IDs (such as your device's Advertising ID). This data is collected solely for advertising and marketing purposes to serve relevant ads.

In-App Purchases (Premium)

We offer optional in-app purchases to unlock premium features and remove advertisements. All payment processing is handled securely by third-party billing providers (such as Huawei In-App Purchases and other respective platform providers). We do not collect or store your credit card details or billing information on our servers.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device. Link to the privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the app:

Data Security (Encryption in Transit)

We value your trust. Any data collected and transmitted by our third-party advertising partners (such as Device IDs) is transferred over a secure, encrypted connection (Encrypted in transit) to protect your information.

Network Data & Ping Functionality

The core functionality of Z Pinger involves sending lightweight HTTP HEAD requests (Pings) to specified servers (e.g., Google DNS, ISP domains) to prevent the network radio from entering idle mode.

  • These requests are technical signals only.
  • No personal user data is transmitted in these requests.
  • You have full control to start/stop this service at any time.

Foreground Service Permission

To function correctly, Z Pinger uses a Foreground Service (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE). Why is this needed? The Android operating system automatically suspends background apps to save battery. To keep your internet connection active, Z Pinger must run continuously in the background. Transparency: A persistent notification is displayed in your status bar whenever the service is active, ensuring you are always aware that the app is running.

Log Data

The app displays a local "Log Console" to show you the status of connection pings. These logs are stored temporarily in your device's RAM and are cleared when you close the app or click the delete button. We do not upload these logs to any external server.

Data Deletion & Subject Rights

Because Z Pinger does not require users to create an account, and we do not store personal data on our servers, there is no separate data deletion request process provided. Users can request clarification or exercise their privacy rights by contacting us directly.

Children’s Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to do the necessary actions.
